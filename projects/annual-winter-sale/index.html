The short version

Generation is a Pakistani women’s fast fashion brand that makes an impactful statement every year with its annual summer and winter sales. The brand was looking to advertise its sale event across various print and digital mediums including in-store displays. Drawing inspiration from “Russian Constructivism”, I designed a series of digital and print artwork that multiplied sales by 2.5x.

01 / ContextAbout the client

Generation is a women’s fast fashion brand based in Pakistan. The brand became an answer to the rising demand by Pakistani urban women for affordable ready-to-wear clothes that celebrate tradition with creativity & innovation.

02 / The challengeWhat needed solving

Every year, Generation has its Annual Summer and Annual Winter Sales through which it attracts new and existing customers. The brand wanted to create an Annual Winter Sale campaign according to its brand requirements which included the use of Generation’s signature red colour and “Avant Garde” as its main font. The client wanted the design to look “bold”, “striking” and “masculine”. The primary design outcome would be newspaper advertisements, web banners, social media posts and in-store graphics.

03 / My roleWhat I did

As the graphic designer I was tasked with developing the campaign. I researched on various design references and took them as inspiration to create the art direction for the main artwork of the newspaper advertisements. The main artwork was used as a base to create the rest of the campaign.

04 / ApproachHow I worked it out

The art style and direction was inspired by the “Russian Constructivism” movement from the 1920s and 1930s. I had researched on the artwork from that era and found that it aligned well with what Generation required from their campaign. The historical artwork helped inspire the shapes, patterns, design elements and compositions for the campaign artwork. For the advertisements, I also had to make sure that all the relevant information was communicated clearly.

05 / ResultsWhat happened

The outcome of the campaign included: 2 newspaper advertisements, web banners, and approximately 35 artworks for in-store graphics across 11 locations. During the campaign, sales increased by 2.5x and an average of PKR 1,500,000 was generated in sales every day.
